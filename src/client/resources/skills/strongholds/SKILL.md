---
name: strongholds
description: Finding the stronghold and End portal: eyes of ender, locate_stronghold, digging down, enter_end_portal.
---

The End portal is in a stronghold's portal room, underground in the overworld. Each eye of ender is one ender pearl (collect_ender_pearls; see the ender-pearls skill) and one blaze powder (see the blaze-rods skill); the portal needs up to 12 eyes in its frames (some frames generate filled), and locate_stronghold spends about one in five throws, so bring several spare. Do not throw eyes by hand: the flight is too quick to read between turns. Call locate_stronghold from the surface in the overworld.

locate_stronghold ends with stronghold_located estimate=x,z (confirmed=false means no eye was left to check it near the spot). Eyes lead to the stronghold's starting staircase, under that column, usually between y=10 and y=50; its corridors spread tens of blocks around it. Mark the spot with remember_place, then navigate_to the estimate with a deep y (about 30): navigation digs a staircase down, never a straight shaft. Look for stone bricks. Once inside, use find_world_features featureKind=end_portal to locate exposed portal frames in loaded chunks; walk the corridors toward stone brick and cracked/mossy variants if it returns nothing yet. Walk into the portal room (frames in sight), then call enter_end_portal: it fills every empty frame with an eye, checks the portal lit and walks in. Do not fill frames with use_block one by one: they hang over a lava pool. Before calling it, prepare for the dragon fight (fight_ender_dragon: bow and arrows, building blocks, food, armor); with enter=false it only lights the portal so you can get ready first.

On failure the reason carries best_estimate and the bearings so far: no_eyes_of_ender or out_of_eyes (craft more and call again from the last throw point), bearing_inconclusive (raise maxEyes or move a few hundred blocks and call again), route_blocked (walk around the obstacle with navigate_to, then call again), wrong_dimension (strongholds exist only in the overworld), no_eye_flight (no stronghold the eye can lead to).

enter_end_portal fails with a reason: not_enough_eyes need=n have=m (no eye was used; craft the difference and call again), no_portal_frame (no frame in sight: walk closer, or pass x,y,z from find_world_features), incomplete_frame (a player-built ring with frames missing or facing out), unreachable (no standable cell beside the ring: clear or build ground next to it), frame_not_filled or portal_not_lit (look at the frames with inspect_world and call again). After entered_end you stand on the obsidian platform in the End: call fight_ender_dragon.
