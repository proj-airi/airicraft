name: positions-and-placement
description: Remembering places, check_position, check_interaction, placement faces and construction heights.
---

Remember useful positions with name and purpose before leaving. Recall coordinates for known destinations and verify the dimension; stored places are not fresh geometry. For precise interactions, obtain current target evidence and copy exact identifiers/confirmation tokens. inspect_world check_position reports support and feet/head clearance, including partial blocks. check_interaction reports reach, LOS, obstruction and local approach positions; these are local feasibility facts, not guaranteed routes. Door passage depends on collision geometry, not its open flag. For construction label floor-block Y, feet height and roof height: a full floor at Y supports feet at Y+1, with a full roof no lower than Y+3 for standing clearance. Partial blocks need their actual support height. For placement, an explicit facePreference restricts the support direction from the target; down requires support below, not any adjacent solid block. Prefer auto. Reach does not prove visibility of the clicked face: roofs can hide faces below them. After target_not_visible, inspect the obstruction and approach before retrying. attemptedStandPositions=0 means no candidate passed the local stance checks, not that the current position was proven usable.
