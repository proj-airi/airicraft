---
name: beds
description: Beds: a respawn point near your work so a death costs a short walk, and sleeping through the night instead of travelling in it.
---

A death drops everything and sends you back to the world spawn, which can be a thousand blocks from your base or portal. Set your respawn point early, as soon as you have a base or a portal you will come back to.

Make a bed from three wool and three planks of one kind at a crafting table. Wool comes from sheep: kill three (each drops one) or shear them with shears (two iron ingots). Wool colours do not need to match each other.

Place the bed on flat ground near your base or portal with two free cells in a row, then use_block on it. Sleeping in it sets your respawn point; at night it also skips to morning, which is the safest way to get through a night on the surface. You cannot sleep with a hostile mob within about eight blocks ("You may not rest now, there are monsters nearby"): deal with it or wall the bed in first. Using the bed by day may only say you can sleep at night; check the chat message for "Respawn point set". Carry the bed with you on a long trip (break it, it drops itself) and sleep at dusk wherever you are, walled in by a few blocks.

Never use a bed in the Nether or the End: it explodes. If the bed is broken or blocked when you die, you respawn at the world spawn again.
