---
name: mining-light
description: Torches, charcoal and a shield before dark or dangerous mining.
---

When mining requires illumination, obtain torches first. Coal and sticks craft torches directly; do not burn coal to make charcoal for torches. Without coal, smelt logs into minecraft:charcoal using spare planks or sticks, reserving the logs needed as input. Then craft minecraft:torch from charcoal and sticks. Prefer carried fuel and short crafting steps over a fuel-mining trip. Automatic lighting starts enabled for dark underground mining and navigation; configure_lighting can change or disable it. Use allowUnilluminated only with explicit user permission. Before dangerous exploration, prefer one shield when supplies allow (one iron ingot, six planks and a workbench), then equip it in offhand. Do not interrupt combat to craft or repeatedly obtain an already carried shield. System 1 blocks toward the shooting skeleton.
